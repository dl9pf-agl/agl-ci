AGL CI downloads

release/
Released images, by release line and version: release/<branch>/<version>/. Signed by the CI and by a release engineer; check SHA256SUMS and its signatures.
staging/
Releases that were just built and are waiting for the engineer's signature. Not verified yet: do not use them.
nightly/
Nightly builds, one folder per image: nightly/<date>/<repo>/<machine>/<target>/, with <target>.SHA256SUMS, build.toml, report-trowl/ and report-vulnscout/. Not signed, not verified, removed after a few days. <date>/COMPLETE says that every image of that run is there.
