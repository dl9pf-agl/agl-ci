AGL CI downloads

release/
Released images, by release line and version: release/<branch>/<version>/. Signed by the CI and by a release engineer; check SHA256SUMS and its signatures.
staging/
Releases that were just built and are waiting for the engineer's signature. Not verified yet: do not use them.
nightly/
Nightly builds, by date: nightly/<date>/<repo>/<machine>/deploy/images/..., each with a trowl-report/ next to the image. Best-effort, never signed, pruned after a few days.
